MakeEzi

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

MakeEzi is a desktop application that runs entirely on your own computer. This policy explains the little it does, in plain terms.

What MakeEzi does not do

  • It does not require an account, sign-in, or internet connection to use.
  • It does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.
  • It does not include analytics, telemetry, or crash reporting.
  • It does not access your files, folders, or other applications except when you explicitly choose to open or save a file through its own dialogs.

Your projects and files

Models, pictures, and settings you create or save stay on your own device, in locations you choose. MakeEzi never uploads them anywhere.

The one thing it checks online

On startup, MakeEzi makes a single outgoing request to a small, public text file (hosted on GitHub) to see whether a newer version is available. This request does not include your name, files, or any identifying information — it is the same plain request your browser makes to load any public web page. You can also trigger this check manually from the app's menu.

Payment information

If you purchase MakeEzi through a storefront (such as the Microsoft Store), your payment is handled entirely by that storefront under its own privacy policy. MakeEzi itself never sees or stores your payment details.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent via the contact details on makeezi.eu.
