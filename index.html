Everything you need to go from idea to model

A full modelling toolkit — create, reshape, cut, sculpt, finish — with instant feedback as you work.

Sketch, Revolve, Loft, Sweep

Draw a 2D profile and extrude it, spin it around an axis, blend between shapes at different heights, or sweep a cross-section along a path — pipes, tubes and rails included.

Pull — push/pull any face

Click a flat face and drag it in or out live, with an optional chamfer on the new walls. Real topology editing, not a trick.

Bend, Twist, Deform, Hollow

Reshape a finished model non-destructively, then clean up and re-smooth the mesh with Remesh.

Combine & Cut & Join

Union, subtract or intersect two models, cut holes and grooves, or split a model apart.

Sculpt brushes

Push, pull and smooth fine detail into dense meshes by hand — carve faces, seams and organic shapes.

Materials, Pictures & Lighting

Metals, plastics and patterns with roughness/metalness sliders; a built-in label maker and texture tools; lighting presets; render to a finished PNG (oil, watercolour, pencil, comic, outline).

Parametric generators

Flanges, gears with a true involute profile, extruded profiles (hex, slot, L-bracket and more) with bevels — all driven by real dimensions.

Knurl & grip patterns

Press diamond, straight, ring or spiral knurling into any flat or round surface.

Import, export & share

Import/export STL, OBJ and GLB. Save a whole project to reopen later, or share a model as a single self-contained web page — no install needed to view it.

Real hardware, ready to 3D print

A full library of nuts, bolts, screws and screw heads at real sizes (M3–M10 and more), so the kits you build actually go together — with print clearances worked out for you.

Pulley with weight

Bracket, wheel on a sleeve, a real M5 screw and nut as the axle.

Planetary gearbox

18 parts, real M3 screws and nuts, a threaded knob.

Four-cylinder engine

Built-up crankshaft, rods, pistons, block with windows — real M3/M6 hardware.

Bench vise

Three printed parts plus real M8 and M10 screws and nuts.

Chain drive

28 identical snap-together links, two toothed wheels and a crank.

Scissor jack

Base, saddle, eight arms, two blocks and a crank.

Steampunk desk fan

Wood, bronze, brass and steel parts with moving blades — every part editable.

…and more

Suitcases, robots, drums, lamps, chairs, phone stands and a sculpted skull — open Add → Ready-made examples in the app.
